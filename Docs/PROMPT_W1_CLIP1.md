## What Franco killed in my draft, and why

**1. The camera could teleport.** *"'Close on her feet' does not establish which side of her the camera
occupies. SD2 could begin beside or in front of the board, then jump behind when Phase 2 begins."* Fixed by
registering the rear axis and the lateral offset in the FIRST FRAME, then describing the rise as a physical
pedestal on that same axis. Same failure he found hidden at ~5.35s in the studied 4K clip.

**2. "Swings up onto the tail" is the pop stance.** *"'Onto the tail' creates a loaded pop position. That is
precisely the body preparation for an ollie or kickflip."* I wrote the body position for the trick I was
trying to forbid. Replaced with *"returns to the deck and settles naturally over the rear bolts."*

**3. Naming the forbidden trick may summon it.** *"Negative language is not dependable enough, and naming
the exact motion can seed it. The accidental test already showed SD2's skate prior wants to produce a pop."*
`no trick, no kickflip, no ollie, no jump` → **all four wheels remain in continuous contact with the asphalt
through the final frame.** Same law as "negation summons the noun", now confirmed in a second domain.

**4. The push phase was too long.** 1.5s *"may encourage SD2 to invent repeated pushes."* Pinned to 0.8s.

**5. My lighting was physically contradictory.** *"Late-afternoon sun is not high. Choose ***REMOVED***"* A causal
block that names an impossible sun. Corrected to a rear three-quarter angle with long clean shadows.

**6. The board underside was under-specified for 4K.** *"'A purple-teal underside with a gold lotus motif'
leaves a lot of unused visual territory, and the model may fill that territory with its learned skateboard
graphics."* The carve is exactly when the underside rotates toward camera. Occupied the whole surface
positively instead — full-bleed field, lotus spanning most of the deck length.
