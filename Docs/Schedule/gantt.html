Why the pipeline gained two stages tonight. Research confirmed Seedance 2.0 accepts a first frame AND a last frame and interpolates between them, rather than extrapolating forward from one image. So the storyboard, the key stills and the video input are the same artifact at three levels of finish — rough sketch, real photograph, generation input. Boarding 48 frames is not extra work ahead of the stills; it is the stills, drafted. The animatic between them costs nothing and is the only thing that proves all six handoffs before money is spent.

Cut 1 → 2 Airborne. The apex completes as her landing. Hard match.
Cut 2 → 3 Forward run. Masked — market fabric, full frame.
Cut 3 → 4 Arms extended. Firing becomes hands on the wheel.
Cut 4 → 5 Rotation. The drift's spin becomes her turning to face him.
Cut 5 → 6 Dissolve. Particles fill frame and clear onto the aisle.
Cut 6 → end Continuous. The world changes under her stride.
The law this reel runs on — generate the consequence, skip the mechanism. The apex, not the full kickflip. The light a gunshot makes, not the gunshot. Her firing then the spin-out, not two tyres deflating on cue. Her face steady while the world rotates past the window, not a car rotating while translating. Four shots reached this independently before it had a name.

The dissonance had a number. Six QA gates existed and every one measured time — flams, density, syncopation, two-bar shape. None measured pitch. The spine's bass sits on F; skate and sword were generating on B, a tritone away, the most dissonant interval in Western music. Uploading the spine into Suno and naming the root moved skate from B to F and its flams from 43% to 0.0%. Sword is the last one still carrying it.
