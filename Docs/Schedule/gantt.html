The tripwire is the point. Eight focused hours on any single shot forces replacement or deletion. Two consecutive sessions with no accepted asset triggers the same. This has to be automatic, because the operator will always believe the next prompt contains the missing magic word. That belief is exactly what cost two weeks last time.

Stage gates

Gate
Passes when
Kill or redesign when
G0 · Pipeline
One face-readable clip survives stills → video → upscale → edit → both aspect ratios, inside one normal session.
Two workflow revisions still damage identity, or vertical delivery stays impractical.
G1 · Identity
Blind testers consistently read one woman across all six wardrobes, with no single world repeatedly called out as someone else.
One full weekend and two materially different identity systems both fail. Then continuity is redesigned around recognition rather than facial replication.
G2 · Risk shots
Every world has one usable clip at its intended edit length, with acceptable identity, motion and crop flexibility.
No keeper after two focused sessions, roughly six active hours. Simplify or replace the shot.
G3 · Rough cut
All six worlds, the full musical structure and the vertical extraction work with real footage.
The cut depends on several future miracle shots. Reduce scope or drop a world.
Picture lock
No placeholders. Every remaining problem is solvable by editing, grading, sound, or a defined replacement.
Week 14. Ship the best existing version regardless of state.

What can run in parallel

Start now, no dependencies

  • Music. Skeleton is d***REMOVED*** Six variations need nothing from the visual side.
  • Body sheet and hair lock. Gates every wardrobe sheet, so it is first.
  • Props. Sword, sidearm and board depend on nothing.
  • Environment plates. Empty locations, no character, no blocker.
  • Case study capture. Only exists if recorded live.

Parallel after Gate 1

  • All six worlds at once. Once identity passes, every world's risk shot is independent.
  • One agent per world is the natural split — same spec, six lanes, no shared state.
  • Coverage parallelizes the same way after Gate 3.

Strictly serial

  • Pipeline proof → everything. No point generating into an unproven workflow.
  • Body sheet → wardrobe sheets. Wardrobe inherits proportions.
  • Track lock → transitions. Transitions are timed against real audio, not theory.
  • Transitions → coverage. A transition that fails changes the shots either side of it.
  • Vertical designed at week 5, cut at week 11. Designing it late produces a shorter edit with missing rhythm.
