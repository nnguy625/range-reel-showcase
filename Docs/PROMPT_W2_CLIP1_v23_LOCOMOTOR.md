## WHAT CHANGED FROM v22, AND WHY

Franco watched A-12 sighted (fetched through the Drive connector, audio in sync) and ruled.

**His verdict:** *"A-12 does not read as constant-energy. It reads as continuous travel interrupted by
transported poses."*
- 0.46s — landing has real energy, rebound starts immediately.
- **1.50–2.50s — "posing z***REMOVED***"** Small forward steps while carrying nearly the same hand-and-torso
  arrangement. Motion exists, the choreography does not evolve.
- **3.86–4.61s — the drum entry fails to change the attack.** Arms broaden; the body does not cut,
  snap or redirect.
- 6.50–7.50s — strongest section; overhead pathway, turn, advancing feet, skirt momentum.

### 1. 🔴 HE DROPPED THE CALM-CENTRE THESIS
> "In this pipeline, written restraint on the lead repeatedly becomes missing choreography. Paola
> should be the **causal centre, not the least-active centre**: she initiates every pathway, performs
> its largest and clearest version, and the ensemble catches her accent. **Authority comes from
> leading the motion, not withholding it.**"

This reverses his own msg-58 design ("I barely need to move my body to own the whole frame") and
settles the four-round argument in Nelson's favour.

### 2. 🔴 THE ACTUAL BUG — NO LOCOMOTOR ENGINE
He confirmed sustained-vs-percussive is real dance theory, with the correction that IS the fix:
> "Constant energy is less useful than **constant kinetic activity**… **A slow arm sweep over passive
> feet would still be posing, so sustained passages require an active locomotor engine underneath.**"

**v22 had exactly that defect** — under the strings the arms carried the phrase while the feet went
quiet. That is the 1.50–2.50 posing z***REMOVED*** In v23 the feet are the engine for all 8 seconds; the arms
ride on top.

**The usable law:** *strings carry motion through space; percussion cuts that moving energy into
defined shapes.*

### 3. THE DRUM WINDOW — PERCUSSION IS THE HINGE, NOT THE CONTAINER
0.75s cannot hold a 1.875s four-count phrase. He confirmed the trigger structure and refined it:
**single, single begin UNDER the strings → the drum entry lands on the faster double, double → the
momentum carries through the returning strings.**

### 4. "NO REPEATS" SOLVED
> "The rhythmic signature repeats, but **each left-right pair changes movement vocabulary.**"

The rhythm (SINGLE SINGLE DOUBLE DOUBLE) is identical every phrase — that is the learnable TikTok
hook. The movement inside it is new every phrase — that is Nelson's "no repeats." Both, not either.

### 5. BEAT ARITHMETIC — IT FITS
4 phrases × 4 counts = 16 counts. At 128 BPM one count = 0.469s → 7.50s, plus the landing at 0.46s
≈ 7.96s. The structure is beat-exact for an 8-second clip.
