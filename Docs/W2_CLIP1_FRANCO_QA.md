## MY READ — the editorial slack Franco does not have

His verdict assumes frame-one purity is pass/fail. It is not.

- Editorial window for W2 clip 1 = **7.500s**. Generation = **8.000s**.
- **Slack = 0.500s = 12 frames at 24fps.**

If the world takes 3–8 frames to fully swap, that smear gets **trimmed off the head** and never reaches
the cut. The fire only fails if the swap takes longer than half a second or the geometry is lost outright.

So v2 stops demanding an *instantaneous* swap (which A2 proves is self-contradictory) and demands a **fast**
one — "within the opening handful of frames." That is honest, executable, and dissolves A2/A3/A4 as written
defects while leaving A1/A5 as the real gamble.
