# THE DIRECTING SYSTEM — "Embodied Kinetic Escalation"
### Franco's reverse-engineering of Nelson's own instinct, 2026-07-28. This is the through-line for every remaining world.

Nelson asked Franco to *"reverse engineer that directive instinct"* after rejecting a clean, readable
left-to-right profile for Shot 1 in favour of a low rear-tracking push-off. Franco's answer is the most
portable thing in the whole thread: it turns taste into a **procedure**, so Worlds 3–6 can be derived
instead of re-argued.

## The decision order

Nelson does **not** pick the clearest angle first. He picks the angle with the strongest bodily
sensation, then solves clarity around it.

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | What should the audience physically **feel**? |
| 2 | Where should the camera **live** to create that feeling? |
| 3 | How should the camera **evolve** through the sequence? |
| 4 | How do we preserve enough screen direction and continuity for the edit to stay legible? |

> *"You do not choose the clearest angle first. You choose the angle with the strongest bodily
> sensation, then solve clarity around it."*

## Progressive revelation — ration the information

We meet Paola through **movement** before we get the clean hero view of her face.
Feet and board → partial profile → full lateral body → face-visible airborne payoff.

> *"You ration information instead of showing everything immediately."*

## Camera position is an arc, not a preference

| Position | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Behind her** | we join her |
| **Beside her** | we experience her speed |
| **In front of her** | we anticipate the trick |
| **Below her** | she becomes iconic |

> *"Your instinct is not merely 'use cooler angles.' It is to make the camera's relationship with
> Paola change as her power grows."*

## The four-move formula — applies to every world

```
inherit motion  →  translate motion into genre language  →  expand into spectacle  →  end on a transformation frame
```

> *"You want every world to preserve the previous motion's energy, then let the new genre absorb it and
> amplify it through its own grammar."*

⚠ **A world never simply ends.** Shot 4 of every world is a **transformation anchor**, not a stop.
The kickflip apex *becomes* Bollywood. The fabric wipe *becomes* the corridor. Do not board a world
whose last panel merely concludes.

## The one caution Franco attached

Starting hidden is right, but **do not stay hidden too long**. By the end of an opening shot, cheat
outward far enough to read Paola as a person, not just legs and board. A partial cheek or profile read
is enough. That buys the skate authenticity *and* the identity.

> *"The rear push-off is the cooler shot. The left-to-right idea is the readability rule. Use both.
> One gives style. One gives clarity."*

# Per-world shot logic — Franco, applying the system

## World 2 · Bollywood — Nelson's brief
> *"Bollywood i want her to match cut land into the scene & immediately dance doing the same hop while
> traveling, w/ coordinate arm & hand movements, while the back up dancers are also syncing their dance
> moves w/ hers so it looks bollywood synchronized."*

**Core instinct: the landing is not a reset, it is a continuation of the airborne kickflip energy. She
does not arrive and then start dancing. She arrives already inside the dance.**

Chain: match-cut continuity → embodied motion → synchronized expansion → spectacle payoff.

| Shot | Bars | Time | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.5 | 15.0000–17.8125 | Lands from the kickflip apex in the **same body geometry and same downward motion**. The landing converts straight into a travelling dance hop in the same direction. **The hop is the bridge.** Feet absorb impact, then the body rebounds into rhythm. Medium-full, slightly low, front 3/4 or side 3/4 — the camera *receives* her arrival rather than observing from a distance. |
| 2 | 2 | 17.8125–21.5625 | **Arms and hands become the star.** Still travelling, still dancing, and now the nearest dancers snap into her choreography — precise pickup, not random crowd dancing. This is the *"she leads, they answer"* shot. Synchronization grows outward from her. The audience feels: **Paola moves, the world obeys.** |
| 3 | 3 | 21.5625–27.1875 | **Full synchronized payoff.** The whole ring locks to her. Not realism first — **spectacle through coordination.** This is where an orbit or sweeping circular move genuinely belongs, because *"Bollywood earns choreographic circularity"* in a way skate does not. Colour, costume and rhythm bloom. |
| 4 | 1.5 | 27.1875–30.0000 | She **breaks forward through the hanging fabric.** Not an exit — another transformation gesture. The fabric wipe becomes the corridor. |

> *"Land into dance, dance becomes leadership, leadership becomes synchronized spectacle, spectacle
> becomes the transition."*

**The lattice corroborates the logic.** World 2's ratified split is **1.5 / 2 / 3 / 1.5**, and the
spectacle shot is the one that gets the 3 bars while the bridge and the wipe get 1.5 each. The rhythm
and the story agree without being forced to.

## World 4 · Car chase — Nelson's brief
> *"The car chase i don't want agent, i want fast & furious visual style & grade."*

Franco: *"This should not feel like Agent Part 2. This is not corridor gun-fu in a car."*
Same escalation rule, but now **the body is fused to the vehicle.**

| Shot | Bars | Time | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 45.0000–48.7500 | **Match from the gun pose into the wheel.** Hands already active — continuity carried through the arms and grip. But the sensation changes immediately from *aiming* to *driving under force*. Camera inside the cabin or very near it: vibration, sunlight, dust, engine urgency. **Kinetic pressure, not corridor precision.** |
| 2 | 1.5 | 48.7500–51.5625 | **Externalize the motion.** Low fast exterior — road-level, wheel-line, or side tracking. The car is not posed, it is **attacking space**. Make us feel acceleration, don't just show a car. |
| 3 | 3 | 51.5625–57.1875 | **The big move** — drift, hard corner, evasive manoeuvre, near miss, threading traffic. World 4's equivalent of the Bollywood spectacle shot. Not a pretty car shot: **the money action beat.** Make the force legible. The viewer should feel *she is controlling chaos*. |
| 4 | 1.5 | 57.1875–60.0000 | **Outgoing transformation.** Something flares, whips, wipes or rotates into the katana world — sunlight streak, dust burst, windshield flare, spinning motion. *"You don't just stop the chase. You convert the motion."* |
