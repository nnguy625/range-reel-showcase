## Why gesture-rhyme cutting is the right architecture here

Every seam is **one continuous body movement completed in a different world.** The viewer's eye tracks
the motion across the cut and the *environment* is what changes underneath it. That is what sells range:
the through-line is her body, the variable is the genre.

It also fixes the reel's structural risk. Six worlds in ninety seconds is a montage; six worlds joined by
completed gestures is a **single continuous performance**. Nothing else in the build does that work.

⚠ **Consequence for production:** every seam needs **two finished frames**, not one shared file — same
geometry, different content (Franco's ruling, `START_END_FRAME_METHOD.md` §3). Wardrobe, prop and
environment change across the cut; **pose, crop, camera, scale, horizon and limb geometry freeze.**
