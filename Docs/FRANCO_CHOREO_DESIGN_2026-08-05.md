## MSG 58 — THE GRANDMASTER-CHOREOGRAPHER DESIGN

**Formula:** Bollywood soul + hip-hop clarity + cinematic staging.
- Bollywood soul = cultural identity: wrists, fingers, mudras, head bobble, shoulder/hip sweetness.
- Hip-hop clarity = viral readability: clean accents, clear stops, repeatable counts, one hook.
- Cinematic staging = depth, crowd design, camera motivation, light reacting to movement.

**Five properties of a viral hook:** one signature movement describable in a sentence · clear count
architecture (one 4-count motif repeated and escalated, *not* eight ideas) · contrast in intensity ·
silhouette readability from medium-wide · social energy (ensemble echoes the rhythm, Paola cleanest).

**His four sections:** entrance/hook plant → hook expansion → percussion hit → exit shape.

**His hook, count by count:**
1. Head bobble begins. High hand frames the face. Low hand opens outward from the waist. Weight
   settles into one hip.
2. Head bobble answers the other side. Wrists turn. Fingers change shape. Feet mark a small shift.
3. A cross-step or side-set carries the body slightly forward/diagonal. Hands redraw the asymmetry.
4. Sharper accent: hip hit, shoulder answer, wrist snap into a clean mudra stop.

Then: first pass small and cool → second pass larger with travel → percussion pass whole body →
exit hardens.

**On SD2 translation (fully aligned with our canon):** SD2 does not respond to dance adjectives
(expressive, flowing, epic, graceful, dynamic) — *"Those are moods. It invents the motion."* Every move
must specify body part, starting state, path, endpoint, rotation/accent, what stops it, what musical
event triggers it.

**On camera:** *"The camera should not choreograph the dance. It should witness and amplify it."*
Avoid over-orbiting — the choreography gets muddy.

**On the W3 handoff:** *"W2 should end like 'watch me'. W3 should begin like 'don't test me.'"*
