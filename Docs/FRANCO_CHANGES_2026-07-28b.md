## 4. 🔨 THE W3 ENVIRONMENT PLATE MUST BE REBUILT — spec addressed to me

> *"Rebuild World 3 as an **action-geography plate**, not merely a mood corridor. The current location
> looks good but gives Paola nowhere to enter, roll, hide, peek, or return fire."*

**One clean 4K master environment plate first — not a storyboard, not a 3×3 sheet, NO characters.**

**Camera:** 16:9 4K photoreal. Front-right foreground, **~90 cm above floor**, looking diagonally across
the corridor toward a rear-left entrance and the deep background firing lane. **28–32 mm** feel.
The frame must show **all four pieces of action geography at once**: entrance · impact wall · roll path ·
cover and attacker lane.

| Zone | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Rear-left / mid-bg entrance** | Doorway with **clear or smoky translucent industrial plastic strip curtains**, flexible enough to burst through. They **echo World 2's hanging fabric but in a colder industrial material.** A little warm light may glow through from behind as a **warm-to-cold bridge**; the corridor stays cold. |
| **Beside the entrance** | A metal doorframe / pipe chase / steel panel / reinforced wall edge that can visibly **take two bullet impacts** — close enough to her path that the audience instantly reads *she is being shot at*. |
| **Movement lane** | Unobstructed **diagonal** path from the curtains toward camera and foreground-left cover, **wide enough for a believable tumble roll**. |
| **Foreground-left cover** | One substantial **waist-high reinforced concrete service barrier**, wide enough to hide her torso and hips crouched, with **one clean side edge to peek and fire from**. ⚠ **Must not block the entrance from camera view.** |
| **Deep background-right attacker zone** | Readable doorway recess / column / loading-bay opening where one attacker stands and fires down the corridor. **The line of fire must be visually obvious.** One dark crate nearby; keep the zone simple. |

**Architecture:** a cold industrial **service concourse**, not an empty hallway — concrete pillars, wall
recesses, exposed conduits, overhead fluorescent practicals, steel surfaces, slightly wet floor. Wide
enough for diagonal movement and cover work, **not so large it becomes a warehouse.** Repeated overhead
lights and strong perspective lines make the firing lane readable.

**Grade:** unchanged — COLD GRAPHITE-CYAN PRECISION THRILLER.

**Continuity logic:** W2 ends with **warm hanging market fabric filling the camera**; W3 opens with
**translucent industrial plastic strips filling the same visual role**, camera already several feet
inside W3 at an offset angle.

⚠ **Avoid:** empty bowling-lane corridor · cover in the centre of the entrance · strips hanging directly
against the camera · giant warehouse · cluttered junkyard · military battlefield · office hallway ·
horror basement · cyberpunk neon · glossy futurism.
