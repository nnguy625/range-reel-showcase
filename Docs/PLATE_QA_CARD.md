## 🔴 THE SEVEN REJECT REASONS — every one is a direct quote

| # | Reject | His words | What it really means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Reads as CGI** | *"she looks cgi or fake"* · *"not realistic"* | The figure is **cleaner and sharper than the room.** Fix is capture-match — grain over her, contact shadows, soft edges, same focus — **never more detail.** |
| **2** | **Unmotivated light** | *"she's brightly lit by unknown light source, makes no sense"* | Every source must be **in frame**. If a fill has no origin, it is a defect, not a style. |
| **3** | **Identity drift** | *"nano pro drifted her face"* | Face must match the anchor. Usually a **pixel-count and angle** problem, not a wording problem. |
| **4** | **Aged her** | *"she looks super older than what paola suppose to look like"* | ⚠ **A separate failure from drift.** A plate can hold identity and still age her. Hard-clinical rendering does this. |
| **5** | **Geography wrong or illogical** | *"wrong wall she's behind"* · *"why would there conveniently be a random block there for this environment?"* | Two tests: is she where the map says, **and does the architecture have a reason to exist.** |
| **6** | **Blocking doesn't read** | *"she's open to take bullets, also she's supposed to take cover"* · *"she's turning sideways towards the wall, she's suppose to be aiming at something in front of her"* | Cover reads only through **occlusion**. Intent must be visible in the body, not implied. |
| **7** | **Over-dramatic** | *"lighting from gun flash super dramatic"* · *"sounds really corny"* (music, same instinct) | He rejects anything that **announces itself.** Restraint reads as expensive; theatricality reads as trying. |

**Standing enemy: BLUE.** Flagged three separate times — *"not really feeling the blue part of the hotel,
looks off"*, *"is there anything in the prompt that makes the hall blue down?"*. Blue is a defect on sight.

## THE GATE — run in this order, stop at the first fail

1. **Identity** — is it her, and is she **her own age**? (two checks, not one)
2. **Geography** — right place, right wall, and does the architecture justify itself?
3. **Blocking** — does the body read the intent? Is cover **occluding**?
4. **Light** — every source in frame? Anything unmotivated?
5. **Realism** — is she cleaner or sharper than the room? Contact shadows present?
6. **Grade** — matches the world's locked block in `GRADES_SIX_WORLDS.md`? Any blue?
7. **Restraint** — does anything announce itself?

**Verdict language:** KEEP · RE-ROLL (same prompt, sibling variance) · FIX-PROMPT (named change) ·
ESCALATE (needs Nelson's ruling, not mine).

⚠ **Judge from four siblings, never ***REMOVED***** Measured on this project: identical wording produced 0.23 and
0.90 on the same gate. A verdict on one take is a coin read. His own instruction: *"generate FOUR."*

⚠ **Three attempts per plate, then stop and change kind** — not a fourth of the same. `HARD RULE 1`.
