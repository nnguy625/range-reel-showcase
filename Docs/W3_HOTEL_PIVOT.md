## 9. THE W3 MONEY SHOT — Franco's paste-ready prompt, parked for SD2 testing

**References:** `@Pao-Agent` (identity + wardrobe authority) · `@Loc-Hotel-Cover` (primary environment) ·
`@Loc-Hotel-Reverse` (geography support only, for line of fire and depth)

**Camera:** low, **80–90 cm**, **35 mm**, three-quarter from slightly behind and to the side of her cover.
Close enough to feel intense, wide enough to read corridor depth and threat direction.

**Composition:** foreground-right is the hotel column/corner she uses as cover. She is crouched tight in
mid-foreground, body mostly concealed, leaning out just enough to peek and fire. Strong three-quarter
profile, **both hands on the pistol**, arms extended with intent. Corridor stretches away so the line of
fire is clear. **Draped entrance visible behind her**, tying the geography together.

**Action:** the instant of **controlled return fire** — *"not spraying wildly, composed, tactical, exact."*
Bright muzzle flash. One knee bent, shoulders engaged, eyes down the hall. **Attacker not prominent** —
threat implied or very distant. *"the peak heroic image of the sequence."*

**Never:** no text · no logo · no watermark · no oversized smoke · no extra people near camera · no goofy
action pose · **no industrial warehouse elements** · no random unestablished props · **no blue hallway
wash** · no attacker beside her · **no front-facing firing toward camera.**

**If it comes back posed, append:** *"Favor natural mid-action tension over poster posing; the body should
feel caught in a real tactical beat, not staged."*
